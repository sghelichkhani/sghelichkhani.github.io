I am a numerical geophysicist and ARC DECRA Fellow at the Research School of Earth Sciences, Australian National University. I build adjoint and inverse methods for mantle convection, glacial isostatic adjustment and continental-scale groundwater, and I am a core developer of the open-source framework [G-ADOPT](/research/g-adopt/).

- **2026 Outstanding Early Career Scientist Award**, Geodynamics Division, European Geosciences Union
- **AUD 13.0 million** total value of grants on which I am an investigator, including AUD 1.06 million as sole investigator (GeoPath counted at its cash contribution only)
- **1,232 citations, h-index 18** ([Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=45YDfF4AAAAJ), October 2026)
- **More than 30 publications**, including two papers in *Nature Geoscience*

## Research Software

- **[G-ADOPT](/research/g-adopt/)**: Geoscientific ADjoint Optimisation PlaTform, a finite-element framework for forward and inverse geodynamics built on Firedrake. Core developer since 2021, with 14 tagged releases (October 2026). Framework paper: [Ghelichkhan et al. (2024), *Geoscientific Model Development*](https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-17-5057-2024). Software DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.5644391](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5644391)
- **Contributions to [Firedrake](https://www.firedrakeproject.org) and [pyadjoint](https://github.com/dolfin-adjoint/pyadjoint)**: merged contributions to the finite-element and automated-adjoint libraries on which G-ADOPT is built
- **[gdrift](/research/g-drift/)**: Geodynamics Data Reformatting and Integration Facilitation Toolkit. DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.19199602](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19199602)
- **[llnltofi](/research/llnltofi/)**: tomographic filtering with the LLNL-G3D-JPS resolution matrix. DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.18796559](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18796559)
- **[srts](/research/srts/)**: tomographic resolution filtering for comparing geodynamic models with seismic observations. DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.18829196](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18829196)
- **OMEGA**: Optimised Modelling of Environmental Groundwater in Australia, terrain-following meshes for groundwater simulation with G-ADOPT. DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23083130](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23083130)
- **PREM-stable**: a gravitationally stable PREM for GIA models (with M. Hoggard and W. Scott). DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23178245](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23178245)
- **ausdem**: Australian elevation data from Geoscience Australia as xarray arrays. DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.20755953](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20755953)
- **austrata**: access to Geoscience Australia borehole and hydrogeology data and Bureau of Meteorology groundwater bore data. DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.20756380](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20756380)
